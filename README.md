## 🛡️ "Wait, Windows Screamed At Me" (SmartScreen & Certificates)

### "Windows protected your PC! Unrecognized app!"
Yeah, don't panic. Dunno isn't a Russian botnet or a crypto miner trying to melt your GPU.

**Here’s the honest truth:**  
Microsoft and Apple demand **$400 to $600 EVERY SINGLE YEAR** for a digital code-signing certificate.  
Do I look like I have $500 lying around to bribe Microsoft just so a lazy blue bean can sigh at you?  
I'm broke. The bean is broke. We can barely afford coffee, let alone enterprise-grade corporate certificates.

Because I didn't pay the corporate extortion fee, Windows Defender SmartScreen automatically panics and treats any indie software like a cyber threat until millions of people download it.

### How to get past the drama:
- **On Windows:**
  1. Click **"More info"** (*Daha fazla bilgi*).
  2. Click **"Run anyway"** (*Yine de çalıştır*).
  3. Congratulations, you outsmarted the $500 paywall.
- **On macOS:**
  1. Right-click Dunno in your Applications folder and click **"Open"**.
  2. If macOS still throws a tantrum: go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **"Open Anyway"**.

### Is Dunno actually safe?
**Yes, 100%.**
- Every release binary is automatically compiled in public on GitHub's secure build servers (CI/CD).
- Dunno has zero malicious intent. Honestly, it barely has the motivation to do its actual job.
- **No spyware:** It doesn't track your screen, doesn't record your keystrokes, and doesn't read your chats. It doesn't even care enough about you to spy on you.

## 🔄 Updates

Once a day, Dunno checks GitHub Releases in the background. If there's an update, it'll casually complain about having to download new lines. It will never force an update on you — you choose when to update (**about.txt > install**).

All update packages are verified with cryptographic keys (Minisign) so nobody can sneak weird binaries onto your computer.

## 🗑️ How to Fire Dunno (Uninstall)

If you get tired of a pixel bean making sarcastic remarks:
- **Windows:** Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Dunno > Uninstall.
- **macOS:** Drag Dunno from Applications to the Trash.
